Introduction
Vehicle SDK Overview
Vehicle SDK provides APIs for retrieving and controlling vehicle information. Third-party apps connect to the Vehicle Service in Pleos Connect through Vehicle SDK. Vehicle Service runs on Android Automotive OS and processes requests from third-party apps to retrieve and control vehicle information through Vehicle SDK APIs.


SDK version
This guide is based on Vehicle SDK v2.3.2 and Vehicle Service v2.3.21.

Entry point and domain structure
Vehicle SDK APIs are provided as domain-specific interfaces, including Brake, CarInfo, Display, Door, DrivingMode, EvBattery, Fuel, HVAC, and Light. Domain objects such as Door and Brake are not instantiated directly. Instead, obtain them through getXxx() methods such as getDoor() and getBrake() on the Vehicle instance, which is the entry point to Vehicle SDK. Use the domain objects to call capability, get, set, and callback APIs. Capability APIs (checkXXXCapabilities) check feature support and the available value range, get APIs retrieve current values, set APIs update values, and callback APIs subscribe to state changes by registering listeners.

Connect to Vehicle Service by calling initialize(), and call release() when you are finished. Vehicle communication uses IPC, so capability, get, and set results are delivered asynchronously through onComplete and onFailed lambdas instead of being returned immediately. Do not call release() before an asynchronous callback completes.

Domain	Description
Brake	Retrieves brake pedal and parking brake states
CarInfo	Retrieves basic vehicle information, such as exterior dimensions, manufacturer, and model year
Display	Retrieves and sets display-related settings, such as the temperature unit
Door	Controls door opening and closing, locking and unlocking, and retrieves door states
DrivingMode	Retrieves drive mode and cruise control information
EvBattery	Retrieves EV battery charging state and energy efficiency
Fuel	Retrieves fuel-related information, such as fuel economy
HVAC	Controls and retrieves heating, ventilation, and air conditioning information, including temperature, fan speed, and humidity
Light	Retrieves light states and controls ambient lighting features
Odometer	Retrieves total mileage, trip distance and duration, and distance units
Safety	Retrieves vehicle warning information
Seat	Controls and retrieves seat positions, angles, and features
SideMirror	Retrieves and sets side-mirror heating states
Steeringwheel	Retrieves and controls steering-wheel states and heating
Tire	Retrieves tire states
Toll	Retrieves and sets ETCS toll-system voice-guidance volume and other settings
TurnSignal	Retrieves turn-signal states
Window	Controls windows and retrieves window states
Wiper	Controls wipers and retrieves wiper states
